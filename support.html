Subscriptions & Billing

Subscription management is handled by the store where the purchase was made.

Apple App Store: Manage or cancel eligible subscriptions from your Apple ID / App Store subscription settings. Purchase restoration, refunds and billing availability are subject to Apple's platform rules.

Google Play: Manage or cancel eligible subscriptions from Google Play subscription settings. Purchase restoration, refunds and billing availability are subject to Google Play's platform rules.

Makan Mana does not provide a separate fake cancellation system for store-billed subscriptions.

Account & Privacy

Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Makan Mana account and associated personal information. If an in-app deletion option is available in your app version, check Profile → Settings / Help & Support → Account or Privacy options → Request Account Deletion.

If you do not see that option, use the existing Makan Mana Privacy Request Centre. The request is reviewed through the existing support/privacy workflow; this page does not directly delete a production account from the browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I manage my subscription?

Use the subscription settings for the store where you purchased it: Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the restore or subscription options available in the app and the applicable store. If the entitlement still does not appear, send a support request with the platform and subscription product involved.

Why does Makan Mana request location access?

Location helps Makan Mana find nearby places, calculate distance and provide area-based food discovery. You control location permission from your device settings.

How do I report incorrect restaurant information?

Select Restaurant / Place Information in the support form and explain what appears to be incorrect.

How do I report inappropriate community content?

Select Community & Content in the support form and provide enough context for the report to be reviewed.

How do I request account deletion?

Use the Privacy Request Centre or the in-app deletion option where available. Some records may be retained where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

How do I contact Makan Mana support?

Use the form above or email hello@makanmana.app.
